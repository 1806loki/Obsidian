

---

## Framing Note

Before each interview, say: _"We're not pitching anything today. We're trying to understand how you run your business. There are no right answers — the more honest, the more useful."_

---

## Business Operations

1. Walk me through exactly how you manage a new client from the day they sign up to their first session.
    
2. What tools are you currently using to run your coaching business? How did you end up with that combination?
    
3. Where does your time go in a typical week? Can you break it down roughly?
    
4. What's the most repetitive thing you do every week that you wish you didn't have to do?
    

---

## Client Management

5. Tell me about the last client you lost. What happened?
    
6. How do you currently know if a client is making progress? Walk me through what you actually look at.
    
7. Have you ever had a client ghost you or go quiet? What did you do, and how did it usually end?
    
8. How do you handle clients who don't follow their plan? What does that conversation look like?
    

---

## Tools & Workflow

9. What does your current setup cost you per month, across all the tools you use?
    
10. Tell me about the last time one of your tools frustrated you. What happened?
    
11. Have you ever tried a new tool and abandoned it? Why did you stop using it?
    
12. If one of your current tools disappeared tomorrow, which one would hurt the most — and which one would you not even notice?
    

---

## Scaling & Growth

13. What's the actual reason you haven't taken on more clients than you have right now?
    
14. How do you currently get new clients? What's working and what isn't?
    
15. Have you ever tried running group programs or cohort-based coaching? How did it go?
    

---

## Payments & Admin

16. How do you currently handle billing and payments? Have you had issues with late or failed payments?
    
17. How much time per week do you spend on things that aren't coaching — admin, chasing clients, updating spreadsheets?
    

---

## Nutrition & Programming

18. Do you give clients diet plans? How do you currently build and deliver them?
    
19. How do you write workout programs right now? How long does it take to build one from scratch?
    
20. When a client's program isn't working, how do you figure that out — and what do you change?
    

---

## Community & Brand

21. Do you have any kind of community around your coaching — a group chat, a Facebook group, anything? How's that going?
    
22. How do your best clients find out about you today?
    

---

## Closing Questions

23. What's the one problem in your business that, if it were solved, would make the biggest difference to your income or your time?
    
24. Have you ever looked for a tool to solve that problem? What did you find?
    
25. Is there anything about running your coaching business that you've just accepted as painful but never found a fix for?
    

---

## What to Listen For

|Signal|What it means|
|---|---|
|They describe a workaround they built themselves|Strong pain signal — they cared enough to solve it|
|They mention a specific dollar cost or time cost|Quantifiable problem worth solving|
|They say _"I just deal with it"_|Resigned pain — validate carefully|
|They can't remember a specific example|Problem may not be real or frequent enough|
|They get animated or frustrated while explaining|Emotional signal — high-priority problem|

---

**Keep each interview under 30 minutes. Record with permission. Never react to answers with "we're building exactly that" — stay neutral and keep probing.**
