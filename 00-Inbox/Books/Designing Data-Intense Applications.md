### Chapter-1 
- Scalability - As the system grows(traffic, volume, data volume, complexity), there should reasonable ways of dealing with it .
- Reliability - The system should continue to work correctly (performing correct functions at the level of desired performance) even in the face of adversity (hardware or software faults or human errors).
- Maintainability - Over time different people will work on the system (engineering and operations, both maintaining current behavior and adapting to new use cases ) and they should be able to do the work productively.
